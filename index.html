About Me

Hi! I’m Keyur, founder of KEYUR™. I build thoughtful digital experiences and creative projects that make people feel hyped, inspired, and sometimes even emotional. I love mixing creativity with problem-solving, exploring tech and art, and turning ideas into things people actually enjoy. From content creation to web development, every project I take on is made with intention, attention to detail, and a little bit of chaos that makes it fun. My goal?
